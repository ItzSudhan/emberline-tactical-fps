Emberline is an original round-based tactical shooter in three.js that runs offline in the browser. It is 5v5 against bots on Kilnworks, an abandoned desert ceramics plant. The Stokers plant the Igniter at Kiln Hall (A) or Silo Court (B), and the Kilnwatch hold the sites and defuse it; the squads swap sides at half time. You get a buy menu and loss-streak economy, six guns, grenades, smokes and flashes. Headshots and recoil matter, and you have to stop moving before you shoot. Bots follow routes, hold angles, hear footsteps and seek cover.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
